[h1]Boss Build Advisor (Auto Cross-Mod)[/h1]

Automatic, modpack-agnostic boss progression build recommender for [b]tModLoader 1.4.4+[/b].
Instead of relying on hardcoded spreadsheets, it automatically inspects every loaded mod at startup and suggests complete boss-by-boss loadouts across vanilla and modded bosses.

[h2]What It Recommends Per Boss Stage[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Weapons & Ammo[/b]: Up to 8 primary + 4 support/utility weapons + 3 ammo options (including Sentry/Whip support and custom mod classes)
[*] [b]Armor Options (#1 / #2 / #3)[/b]: Top 3 selectable full sets or mixed loadouts with defense & set-bonus analysis (including 2-piece wizard robe sets)
[*] [b]Role-Budgeted Accessories (Up to 14)[/b]: Wings, Boots, Dash, Class Boost, Defense/Utility — suppresses redundant crafting precursors (such as Rocket Boots once Spectre/Lightning Boots unlock)
[*] [b]Potions & Buffs[/b]: Class-filtered combat buffs & healing matching the target stage
[*] [b]Obtainability Difficulty Filter[/b]: Switch between [b]Loot: Comfort[/b] (excludes rare RNG farms, shimmer skips, and low-chance crates), [b]Loot: +Hard[/b], and [b]Loot: All RNG[/b] with exact acquisition steps
[/list]

[h2]Cross-Mod Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*] Syncs 1:1 with [b]BossChecklist[/b] (`GetBossInfoDictionary` 2.0.0) ordering, visibility, and defeated checkmarks (with automatic standalone fallback)
[*] Auto-discovers custom mod [b]DamageClasses[/b], custom armor sets, crafting recipes, `RecipeGroup`s, `ItemDropsDB`, NPC shops, and Shimmer transforms
[*] [b]Client-side[/b] (`side = Client`) — safe for singleplayer and multiplayer servers without requiring server installation
[/list]

[h2]Controls & Commands[/h2]
[list]
[*] Press [b]K[/b] (rebindable in Controls, works on pause too) or click the [b]Build Advisor[/b] button next to BossChecklist's book icon in your inventory
[*] Click the boss search bar and type directly (chat does not need to be open)
[*] Chat commands: `/advisor`, `/advisor next`, `/advisor stage <boss>`, `/advisor rebuild`, `/advisor dump`
[/list]

[h2]RU / Русское описание[/h2]
Автоматический советник билдов по боссам для любой сборки модов (порядок и статусы 1:1 как в BossChecklist, модовые классы/броня/боссы, фильтр сложности добычи «Комфорт / +Сложно / Всё RNG», мгновенный поиск без открытия чата). Открывается по клавише [b]K[/b], кнопкой рядом с книжкой BossChecklist в инвентаре или командой `/advisor`.
